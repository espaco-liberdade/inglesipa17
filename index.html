17. Expressando Habilidades e Pedindo Permissão

17.1 Verbos Modais: Uma Introdução 🧩

Verbos Modais (Modal Verbs) são verbos auxiliares especiais em inglês. Eles nos ajudam a expressar ideias como habilidade, possibilidade, permissão, obrigação, etc.

  • Eles não mudam de forma com o sujeito (não adicionamos "-s" para he/she/it).
  • Eles são sempre seguidos pelo verbo principal na forma base (infinitivo sem "to").
  • Para formar negativas, adicionamos "not" depois do verbo modal.
  • Para formar perguntas, invertemos o verbo modal e o sujeito.

17.2 Habilidade no Presente: "Can" (Eu consigo, eu posso) 👍

Usamos "can" para falar sobre habilidades ou capacidades no presente.

17.3 Permissão no Presente: "Can I...?" 🙋

Usado para pedir ou dar permissão de forma informal.

  • Can I use your phone, please?
    (Posso usar seu telefone, por favor?)
  • Yes, you can.
  • No, you can't.
  • Can we sit here?
  • Can I ask a question?

17.4 Habilidade no Passado: "Could" (Eu conseguia, eu podia) 🕰️👍

"Could" é o passado de "can" para falar sobre habilidades gerais no passado.

Afirmativas com "Could"

Frase em Inglês Detalhes (IPA / Tradução)
When I was young, I could run very fast. /wen aɪ wəz jʌŋ aɪ kəd rʌn ˈveri fæst/
(UÉN ái uãz IÂN, ái cãd RÂN VÉ-ri FÉST)Quando eu era jovem, eu conseguia correr muito rápido.
She could speak three languages at ten. Ela conseguia falar três idiomas aos dez anos.
He could play the guitar as a teen. Ele sabia tocar violão quando era adolescente.
We could see the mountains. Nós conseguíamos ver as montanhas.
They could swim across the river. Eles conseguiam atravessar o rio a nado.
I could climb trees easily. Eu conseguia subir em árvores facilmente.
You could see the stars clearly. Você conseguia ver as estrelas nitidamente.
He could memorize long poems. Ele conseguia memorizar longos poemas.
She could draw portraits. Ela sabia desenhar retratos.
We could play outside all day. Nós podíamos brincar lá fora o dia todo.
They could hear the ocean. Eles conseguiam ouvir o oceano.
My grandma could bake the best cakes. Minha avó sabia fazer os melhores bolos.
He could understand the teacher. Ele conseguiu entender o que o professor disse.
I could feel the tension. Eu podia sentir a tensão na sala.
She could remember every detail. Ela conseguia se lembrar de cada detalhe.

They couldn't cook that recipe. Eles não conseguiram cozinhar aquela receita.
I couldn't solve the puzzle. Eu não consegui resolver o quebra-cabeça.
You couldn't see the road. Você não conseguia ver a estrada na neblina.
He couldn't lift the heavy box. Ele não conseguiu levantar a caixa pesada.
She couldn't believe her eyes. Ela não podia acreditar nos seus olhos.
We couldn't get tickets. Nós não conseguimos ingressos para o show.
They couldn't finish the race. Eles não conseguiram terminar a corrida.
I couldn't sleep last night. Eu não consegui dormir na noite passada.

She couldn't come to the phone. Ela não pôde atender ao telefone.
We couldn't agree on a plan. Nós não conseguimos concordar em um plano.

17.5 Pedidos Educados: "Could you...?" 🙏

Usado para pedidos mais educados e formais.

  • Could you help me with this bag, please?
  • Could you pass the salt, please?
  • Could I borrow your pen for a moment?
  • Could you speak a little slower, please?

Tarefa Extra ✍️

Translate the Text

Read the text about John and his grandfather. Then, try to translate it into Portuguese.

John was visiting his grandfather. "Look at this, John," said his grandfather. "When I was your age, I could climb that tall tree in under a minute. I could also swim across the lake. I was very active."

John smiled. "Wow, Grandpa! I can climb trees, but I'm not that fast. And I can swim, but not across a whole lake!"

"Yes, I was. But now, I can't run like I used to, and I certainly can't climb trees anymore. My knees are too old. But I can tell great stories, can't I?"

"Yes, you can!" John agreed. Later, John was having trouble with a piece of a model airplane. "Grandpa, could you help me with this? I can't seem to get this part to fit."

"Of course," his grandfather replied. "I couldn't fix my old watch yesterday because the parts were too small, but I think I can manage this."

John watched as he fixed the piece. "Thanks, Grandpa! Now, can I have a cup of tea, please?"

"Sure! I can make it for you," John said.

Quiz

Based on the text, why couldn't the grandfather fix his watch yesterday?

  1. He didn't have his glasses.
  2. The parts of the watch were too small.
  3. He didn't know how to fix watches.
  4. He was too tired.
  5. John was using his tools.
Ver Resposta do Quiz
Resposta Correta: b) The parts of the watch were too small.
